BRAND SUPERVISION

Brand supervision is an ongoing partnership where we make sure your brand is applied correctly and consistently across all materials and channels.

It helps your team avoid visual drift and fragmented decisions — we stay involved, review what goes out, and keep the brand sharp as your business grows.

The outcome is a brand that stays coherent over time, with a dedicated expert who knows your system and keeps it working.

PROCESS

01 Project onboarding

  • Brief creation
  • Deep dive into the current brand and existing materials
  • Free consultation to align on workflow and expectations
  • Agreement and kick-off after 100% prepayment for the first month

02 Continuous review

  • On-demand review of layouts, presentations, and content
  • Direct channel for fast feedback and expert advice
  • Ensuring consistency across new channels or materials
  • Fixing small errors and adjusting existing templates

03 Sync & Review

  • Regular syncs to discuss the brand's performance and consistency
  • Updating the brand book as the brand evolves
  • Final report at the end of the period with observations
  • Recommendations for growth and scaling

WHAT YOU GET

Ongoing brand quality control across all touchpoints
Fast feedback and edits on materials
A dedicated expert who knows your brand inside out
Brand book updates as the brand evolves
A monthly report with observations and recommendations
