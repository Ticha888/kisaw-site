E-COMMERCE WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT

An e-commerce website is a fully functional online store — built to present your products, guide your customers through the buying journey, and convert visits into sales.

It combines strategic structure, strong design, and reliable development into a store that works seamlessly — from browsing to checkout. Every detail is optimized for trust, clarity, and conversion.

The outcome is a scalable retail platform that captures your brand's aesthetic while providing a flawless shopping experience for your customers.

PROCESS

01 Project introduction

  • Brief creation
  • Free consultation
  • Clarifying the task, workflow format, and Q&A
  • Defining the product range and shop requirements
  • Agreement and kick-off after 100% prepayment for the stage

02 Product Architecture

  • Defining product categories, filters, and attributes
  • User journey mapping: from landing to checkout
  • Inventory and fulfillment logic planning
  • Aligning on the shop functionality and integrations

03 Design & User Journey

  • Developing the UI/UX for product pages, cart, and checkout
  • Visual design for the shop front and editorial sections
  • Optimizing for trust: testimonials, secure badges, clear policies
  • Final design review and approval for development

04 Build & Shop Setup

  • Development and setup of the e-commerce platform
  • Payment gateway and shipping method integrations
  • Product data upload and inventory setup
  • End-to-end testing, launch, and handover documentation
