WHY BONE?

THE IN OUR MARK STANDS FOR STRUCTURE AND FOUNDATION. WE START EVERY PROJECT FROM THE CORE: WE CLARIFY THE BRIEF, BUILD THE BRAND FRAMEWORK, AND DEFINE THE KEY MEANINGS, LOGIC, AND RULES THAT LATER SHAPE THE VISUALS, COPY, WEBSITE, AND ALL TOUCHPOINTS. IT'S A REMINDER THAT THE RESULT IS BUILT ON A SYSTEM, NOT DECORATION.

THE NAME KISAW COMES FROM THE ASSOCIATION OF "KISS" AND "SAW". FOR US, IT DESCRIBES TWO MODES OF WORK: KEEPING THE MEANING INTACT WHILE PRECISELY BREAKING A PROJECT DOWN AND REBUILDING IT. WE TAKE THE RAW INPUT, CUT WHAT'S UNNECESSARY, KEEP WHAT MATTERS, AND TURN IT INTO A CLEAR SYSTEM THE BRAND CAN USE IN REAL LIFE.

PALETTE

OUR CORE PALETTE IS BUILT AROUND BLACK, GREY, AND WHITE AS THE BASE TONES OF PAPER AND INK, THE TOOLS WE USE TO DOCUMENT OUR IDEAS, STEPS, AND STRATEGY.


OUR COLOR ACCENTS WORK LIKE BOOKMARKS IN A BOOK OR A PLANNER. THEY HELP MARK WHAT MATTERS, HIGHLIGHT KEY STAGES, AND KEEP FOCUS ON WHAT NEEDS ATTENTION RIGHT NOW. THE ACCENT IS NOT DECORATION, IT'S NAVIGATION, PRIORITIZATION, AND PROCESS CONTROL.
