REBRANDING

Rebranding is a structured process of rethinking and rebuilding your brand — when your current identity no longer reflects who you are or where you're going.

It helps you realign your brand with new business goals, a changed audience, or a shifted market position. It ensures your visual and verbal communication accurately represents the company you've become.

The outcome is an updated brand system — with clear positioning, a refreshed identity, and guidelines your team can use to move forward with confidence.

PROCESS

01 Project introduction

  • Brief creation
  • Free consultation
  • Clarifying the task, workflow format, and Q&A
  • Defining the goals and objectives the brand rebranding must solve
  • Agreement and kick-off after 100% prepayment for the stage

02 Brand platform

  • Review of the current brand: strengths, weaknesses, gaps
  • Competitor and market analysis: finding the "empty space"
  • Defining new brand positioning and differentiators
  • Formulating brand values, mission, and tone of voice

03 Visual identity

  • Developing the new visual system: logo, color, typography
  • Building the layout logic and graphic elements
  • Stress-testing the new style across primary touchpoints
  • Finalizing the system as a cohesive, future-proof language

WHAT YOU GET

An updated brand platform with positioning and values
Refresh of the visual identity system
Updated brand book with usage guidelines
All source files ready for production
Recommendations for rolling out the rebrand consistently
