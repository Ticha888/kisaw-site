MULTI-PAGE WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT

A multi-page website is a complete digital home for your brand — designed to hold complex information, different audience paths, and a deeper story.

It provides a scalable structure that grows with your business, offering space for services, portfolios, blogs, and detailed brand values. We review what you have, listen to your ideas, and propose a clear direction for how the project should look, sound, and communicate.

The outcome is a comprehensive, professional website that establishes long-term credibility and serves as the central hub for all your marketing efforts.

PROCESS

01 Project introduction

  • Brief creation
  • Free consultation
  • Clarifying the task, workflow format, and Q&A
  • Defining the sitemap and information architecture
  • Agreement and kick-off after 100% prepayment for the stage

02 Analysis & Sitemap

  • Competitor analysis: website structures and features
  • Detailed sitemap development: defining pages and user journeys
  • Content planning: mapping existing materials and identifying gaps
  • Aligning on the full website scope before design

03 Design & Prototyping

  • Developing the UI/UX concept and visual design system
  • Prototyping key pages and interactive elements
  • Responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop
  • Final design review and approval

04 Build & Launch

  • Development with CMS integration for easy content management
  • SEO optimization and performance tuning
  • Testing across all devices and browsers
  • Domain setup, publishing, and handover training
