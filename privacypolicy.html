PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: January 1, 2026

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website.

Information we collect

When you submit a project brief, we collect your name, email address, and project-related information you choose to provide: brand or company name, product description, budget range, timeline, market, and any additional notes. This information is used to evaluate and respond to your project request.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with it. Google Analytics is configured without cookies — no cookies are stored on your device. Analytics data is aggregated and does not directly identify individual visitors. No advertising or third-party tracking is used.

How we use your data

  • To respond to messages and project inquiries you submit through the contact form
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to third parties.

Legal basis for processing

  • Contact form data is processed based on your consent, indicated by the checkbox before submission.
  • Analytics data is processed on the basis of legitimate interest to understand how the site is used and improve its performance. Since no cookies are used, your consent is not required for analytics.

Third-party services

This website uses Google Analytics, provided by Google LLC. Google may process anonymised technical data such as IP address and device information to deliver analytics functionality. For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Data retention

  • Contact form submissions are kept for no longer than 12 months after our last communication, or until your project inquiry is resolved.
  • Analytics data is retained in accordance with Google Analytics default settings (26 months).

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Request a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used format (data portability).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@kisaw.studio.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact me at hello@kisaw.studio.
